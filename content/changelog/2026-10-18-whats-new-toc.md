What's new now has the same **On this page** list as the Learn articles. Every update is listed on the right, and the ones you're reading are highlighted as you scroll. Click a title to jump straight to it. Each update also has its own link you can share, such as `/whats-new#2026-10-17-share-link`.

The list appears when the page area is wide enough to sit beside the updates. On smaller screens the page stays a single column.

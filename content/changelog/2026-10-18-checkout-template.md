The new [Product & Checkout](/templates/checkout) template is the first in a new **Commerce** category. It starts on a product page with an image gallery, a rating, sale pricing, color and size pickers, a quantity input, and shipping details. Adding the product opens a cart drawer, and the checkout step has a validated contact and shipping form, shipping options, a discount code (try TRAIL10), and an order summary.

Storefronts lean on the primary color for prices, selections, and calls to action, so this is a good page for checking that your primary color holds up when it's used a lot.

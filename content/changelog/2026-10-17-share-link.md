Click **Copy share link** in the sidebar toolbar, the export panel, or the command palette. Anyone who opens the link sees your exact theme: colors, shades, neutral, radius, font, and every light and dark override.

The whole theme fits in the URL, so nothing is stored on a server. If a link gets cut off or edited, your current theme stays as it was and a warning explains why. Opening a shared theme can be undone like any other change.

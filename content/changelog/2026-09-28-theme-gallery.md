The new [theme gallery](/themes) collects every built-in theme in one place. Each theme has its own page with a light and dark preview, its `app.config.ts` and CSS ready to copy, and links to related themes and guides.

Search by name, or narrow the list with the category chips, which work the same way they do on the Components, Blocks, Templates, and Learn pages.

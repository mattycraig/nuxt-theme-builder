The homepage now leads with a live preview: real Nuxt UI components and light and dark swatches, all in the theme you're editing. Change a color in the sidebar and the page follows.

Example prompts load a matching theme in one click, and the `app.config.ts` at the bottom of the page is the actual export for your current theme.

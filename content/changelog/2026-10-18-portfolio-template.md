The new [Portfolio](/templates/portfolio) template is a personal site for a design engineer. It has a hero with an availability badge, a scrolling row of tool logos, a filterable grid of selected work, an experience timeline, services, testimonials, recent writing, and a contact form.

It's a more personal, type-led page than the Landing template, which makes it a good test for display fonts and large headings.

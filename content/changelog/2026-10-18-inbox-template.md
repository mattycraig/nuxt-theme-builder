The new [Inbox](/templates/inbox) template is a mail client with three panes: folders and labels on the left, a searchable message list with an All and Unread filter, and a reading pane with a reply box. Unread messages are bold with a dot, the selected one is highlighted, and you can star, archive, or compose a message. On a phone, opening a message replaces the list and a back button returns to it.

Dense lists like this show how your neutral color separates surfaces, borders, and muted text.

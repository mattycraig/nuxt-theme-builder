Neutrals set the tone of a whole interface: backgrounds, borders, and most of the text. Tailwind CSS added four warmer and cooler grays, **Taupe**, **Mauve**, **Mist**, and **Olive**, and you can now pick any of them as your theme's neutral, separately for light and dark mode.

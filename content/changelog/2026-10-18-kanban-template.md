The new [Kanban Board](/templates/kanban) template is a project board with four columns of task cards. Each card shows labels, priority, subtask progress, a due date, and assignees. Drag cards between columns, or use a card's menu to move it with the keyboard. Clicking a card opens a detail panel with status, assignees, a checklist, and activity, and there's a list view and a label filter too.

Labels use your primary, secondary, info, and error colors, and the columns use info, warning, and success, so you can see them side by side on one screen.

Open **Start From a Logo** in the sidebar and pick a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, or SVG. The builder finds the logo's main colors and suggests a primary, a secondary, and a neutral that suits them.

You can swap which color leads, and choose between your logo's exact colors (added as custom palettes) or the closest Tailwind palettes. The image is read in your browser and never uploaded, and applying the colors is a single undo step.

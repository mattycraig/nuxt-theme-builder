The new [Onboarding](/templates/onboarding) template walks through a multi-step signup: create an account, enter a verification code, set up a workspace, choose a plan, and invite your team. A stepper shows where you are, each step validates before you can continue, and the last step confirms you're done.

It joins [Login](/templates/login) in the Auth category and covers the stepper, code entry, and card-style radio options.

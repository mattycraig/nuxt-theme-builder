The new [Booking](/templates/booking) template is a public scheduling page. Pick a date on the calendar, choose an open time (in your time zone, in 12 or 24 hour format), fill in your details, and get a confirmation with add-to-calendar buttons. Weekends and fully booked days can't be picked.

It's the only template that uses the Calendar component, so it's where to check how your theme styles selected, available, and disabled dates.

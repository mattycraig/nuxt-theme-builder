Random themes are a fast way to find new combinations, but until now each roll replaced everything, including the brand color you'd already settled on. Random themes now have their own **Random Theme** section in the sidebar, with a **Generate random theme** button and a **Keep when randomizing** list underneath.

Tick any semantic color (primary, secondary, success, info, warning, error), the neutral, the font, or the radius. A kept color keeps its palette and its shades in both light and dark mode, and everything else is shuffled as usual. **Unlock all** clears the ticks in one click, and each roll can be undone like any other change.

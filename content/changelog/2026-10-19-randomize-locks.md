Random themes are a fast way to find new combinations, but until now each roll replaced everything, including the brand color you'd already settled on. The random theme button in **Theme Presets** is now a split button: the dice half rolls a new theme, and the **lock** half opens a menu where you choose what randomize keeps.

Tick any semantic color (primary, secondary, success, info, warning, error), the neutral, the font, or the radius. A locked color keeps its palette and its shades in both light and dark mode, and everything else is shuffled as usual. The lock half shows how many parts are locked, and **Unlock all** clears them in one click. Each roll can be undone like any other change.

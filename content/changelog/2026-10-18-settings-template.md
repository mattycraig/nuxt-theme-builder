The new [Account Settings](/templates/settings) template is a full settings screen with four tabs. **General** has a validated profile form with an avatar upload, a username field with a prefix, a timezone picker, and a bio. **Notifications** is rows of switches, a digest frequency choice, and checkboxes. **Security** has a password form, two-factor settings, and a list of active sessions you can revoke. **Billing** shows usage meters, a payment method, and an invoice table.

A danger zone at the bottom opens a delete-account confirmation. Inputs, selects, switches, radios, checkboxes, and file upload all sit on one page, so it's the quickest way to see how your theme styles forms.
